What It Is

An integrated circuit is an electronic circuit manufactured as a single physical component.

What It Does

It combines many electronic components, primarily transistors, into one compact circuit.

How to Use

Integrated circuits implement components such as CPUs, memory chips, and controllers.

Requirements

Semiconductor manufacturing and interconnected electronic components.
