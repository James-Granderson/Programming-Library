### What It Is

SATA (Serial ATA) is a hardware interface used primarily to connect storage devices to a computer.

### What It Does

Transfers data between the motherboard and SATA storage devices such as hard disk drives and SATA solid-state drives.

### How to Use

A SATA storage device connects to a compatible SATA port using a SATA data connection.

### Requirements

SATA-compatible hardware
