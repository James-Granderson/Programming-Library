### What It Is

A semiconductor is a material whose electrical conductivity can be controlled between that of a conductor and an insulator. Silicon is widely used in computing; doping and electric fields enable devices such as diodes and transistors.

### What It Does

Semiconductor behavior lets fabricated devices switch or control current, forming the physical basis of digital logic, memory, sensors, and power electronics. A semiconductor material is not itself a complete computer component.

### How to Use

1. Distinguish the material properties from the devices fabricated from it.
2. Trace how a transistor uses voltage or current to control a circuit path.
3. Consult the device or process data for electrical limits and fabrication details.

### Requirements

A suitable semiconductor material and a designed fabrication process are needed to create electronic devices. Properties depend on composition, doping, geometry, and temperature.
