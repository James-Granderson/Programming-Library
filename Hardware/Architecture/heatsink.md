### What It Is

A heatsink is a passive thermal structure, usually metal with a base and fins, that conducts heat away from a component and increases surface area for heat transfer. A fan may move air across it but is a separate active component.

### What It Does

It spreads heat from a hot device into surrounding air or another cooling system. Its effectiveness depends on contact, thermal interface material, airflow, geometry, and ambient temperature; it does not generate cooling by itself.

### How to Use

1. Mount a heatsink with the specified contact pressure and thermal interface material.
2. Ensure the airflow path and fan orientation match the system design.
3. Check temperatures under representative load and verify that the cooler is rated for the component.

### Requirements

A suitable mounting interface, secure attachment, thermal interface material where specified, and a heat-removal path are required.

### Representation

```text
Component → thermal interface → heatsink base → fins → surrounding airflow
```
