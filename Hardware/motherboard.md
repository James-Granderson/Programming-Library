What It Is

The motherboard is the primary circuit board that connects the major hardware components of a computer.

What It Does

Provides physical connections and electrical pathways between the CPU, memory, storage interfaces, expansion devices, power delivery, and other components.

How to Use

Computer components are installed onto or connected to the motherboard.
