What It Is

Fetch-decode-execute is a simplified model of how a CPU processes instructions.

What It Does

Fetches an instruction from memory, determines what the instruction means, performs the required operation, and continues to the next instruction.

How to Use

The CPU repeatedly performs these stages during protion, although modern processors may overlap or reorder them.
