What It Is

PCIe (Peripheral Component Interconnect Express) is a high-speed hardware interface used to connect expansion devicesmputer.

What It Does

Provides high-bandwidth communication between the CPU or chipset and devices such as GPUs, NVMe SSDs, and expansion cards.

How to Use

A PCIe device connects to a compatible PCIe slot or interface.
