### What It Is

A hardware communication pathway used to transfer data, addresses, or control signals between computer components.

### What It Does

Connects components such as the CPU, memory, storage controllers, and other devices so they can exchange information.

### How to Use

A bus is used automatically by hardware components when they communicate.
