### What It Is

A power supply converts electrical power from an external source into the voltages required by computer components.

### What It Does

Provides regulated electrical power to components such as the motherboard, CPU, GPU, storage, and cooling devices.

### How to Use

The power supply connects to the computer's power input and distributes power through the appropriate power connectors.

### Requirements

Compatible power supply and electrical source
