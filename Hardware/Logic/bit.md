### What It Is

A bit is a binary digit with one of two logical values, 0 or 1. In a physical circuit these values are represented by ranges of voltage or another signal, not necessarily by exact zero and one voltages.

### What It Does

Bits encode data, instructions, and control state. Grouping bits forms values such as bytes and words; their interpretation depends on the encoding and context.

### How to Use

1. Determine how many bits the value contains.
2. Interpret their ordering and encoding (for example unsigned binary or two's complement).
3. Distinguish logical bit values from physical signal levels.

### Requirements

A binary representation convention and a medium or circuit capable of distinguishing two states are required.
