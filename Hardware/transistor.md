What It Is

A transistor is a semiconductor device used to control electrical current.

What It Does

In digital computers, transistors act primarily as switches representing binary states.

How to Use

Transistors are combined to construct logic gates and larger circuits.

Requirements

Semiconductor material and electrical circuitry.

Representation
