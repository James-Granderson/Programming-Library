### What It Is

An adder is a digital circuit that performs binary addition.

### What It Does

A half adder combines two bits and produces a sum and carry.

A full adder also accepts an incoming carry.

Full adders can be combined to add multi-bit numbers.

### How to Use

Adders form part of the arithmetic circuitry used by processors.

### Requirements

Logic gates capable of implementing binary addition.
