t It Is

A heatsink is a thermal component that transfers heat away from a processor or other electronic component.

What It Does

Increases the surface area available for heat transfer so heat can move from the component into the surrounding air.

How to Use

A heatsink is mounted directly to a heat-producing component, usually with a thermal interface material between the surfaces.

Requirements

Physical contact with the heat-producing component
