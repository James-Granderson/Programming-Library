What It Is

A computer hardware diagram represents the major physical components and their relationships.

What It Does

It shows how processing, memory, storage, and other components fit together.

How to Use

Use the diagram to reason from a high-level computer down into individual components.

Requirements

Knowledge of the represented hardware.
