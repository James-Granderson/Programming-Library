### What It Is

A NAND gate is an AND gate followed by a NOT operation.

### What It Does

It produces `0` only when all inputs are `1`.

### How to Use

NAND gates can be combined to construct other logic gates and digital circuits.

### Requirements

Digital logic circuitry.
