### What It Is

A hierarchical collection of nodes connected from a root to children.

### What It Does

Represents parent-child relationships.

### How to Use

Start at the root and visit child subtrees recursively or with an explicit stack.

### Representation

```text
        root
       /    \
   child  child
```
